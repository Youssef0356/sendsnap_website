Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Version 2.2.5

Welcome to SendSnap (referred to as "we", "our", or "the App"), developed by The 356 Company. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding user data, permissions, and advertising compliance in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations.

1. Core Philosophy: 100% Peer-to-Peer & Offline Localism

SendSnap is architected from the ground up as a decentralized, direct peer-to-peer (P2P) file transfer tool. We do not maintain cloud servers that store, inspect, relay, or duplicate your files.

When you transfer files, documents, photos, audio, or videos, the data moves straight from your device to the target device across your local Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot. Your files never pass through our servers or any intermediate cloud infrastructure.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No User Accounts: We do not require you to register, create an account, or log in.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email address, or billing info.
  • No File Inspection or Metadata Logging: We do not analyze the filenames, contents, or metadata of the items you share.

3. Android Permissions & Purpose of Use

To enable offline discovery and transfer capabilities on modern Android operating systems, SendSnap requests specific runtime permissions. Each permission is used solely for core functionality:

A. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Used exclusively to scan the receiver's encrypted on-screen QR code during the device pairing sequence. The camera feed is processed in real time entirely on your device. SendSnap does not capture, save, record, or transmit photos or videos from your camera.

B. Storage & Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

SendSnap requires access to device storage to allow you to select photos, videos, music, and documents to send, as well as to write and save received files to your device's download folder. We only access files you explicitly choose to transfer.

C. Wi-Fi & Network State (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES)

Required to detect local network configuration, discover nearby peer devices running SendSnap on the same local subnet, and establish the direct high-speed HTTP streaming socket.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Advertising ID (AD_ID)

SendSnap uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display in-app advertisements that support ongoing development and maintenance. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information, including:

  • The Google Advertising Identifier (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for personalized or non-personalized advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Coarse device parameters (device model, operating system version, carrier, and general location).

For more details on how Google manages and protects ad data, please review the Google Privacy Policy & Terms.

5. Data Security

Because SendSnap operates exclusively over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot connection, your files remain within your physical radio perimeter. We recommend initiating file transfers over private, password-secured Wi-Fi networks or direct personal hotspots.

6. Children's Privacy

SendSnap does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility for local file management and transfer.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
