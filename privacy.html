Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2026

Welcome to SendSnap - Fast Peer-to-Peer File Transfer. Your privacy is our highest priority. This policy detail explains how we protect your data.

1. Data Localism & Sharing

SendSnap is built on a 100% Peer-to-Peer (P2P) engine. This means your files never leave your local WiFi network. We do not have servers that store your files; your data moves directly from your device to the device you choose.

2. Collection of Information

Personal Data

We do not collect name, email, or any identifiable personal information. The "Display Name" you set in the app is stored only on your device and the devices you connect with locally.

Usage Data & Ads

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect certain data, such as your Device ID and approximate location, to serve relevant advertisements and measure performance. You can find Google’s Privacy Policy here.

3. Permissions

To function correctly, the app requires access to:

  • Nearby Devices / Bluetooth: To discover and connect to peers.
  • Location: Required by Android system for WiFi/Bluetooth discovery.
  • Storage: To select the files you want to share and save received files.

4. Security

As a local-only sharing app, your security is tied to your local WiFi network. We recommend sharing only on trusted, password-protected networks.
